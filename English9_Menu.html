An English 9 collaboration. Students use a single OneWord as a critical lens for Romeo & Juliet — and as the seed for a story or poem that goes to a child recovering from cleft lip and palate surgery through Operation Smiles.

The Activity

One word. Two audiences. A bridge between them.

In this unit, every student is assigned one of 25 OneWords — words like PERSIST, BLOOM, FLOW, ANCHOR, RENEW. The word becomes a lens. Students look through it twice: first at the book they have been studying, then at a child they will never meet.

The literary analysis stays in school. The coloring page goes in a smile bag, headed to a child in recovery from surgery. The word is the only thing that links the two pieces — which is exactly the point.
