Section 03 · The Lexicon

The 25 OneWords

Each word lives in two worlds at once — in the natural systems students study, and in the inner lives students navigate every day.

At the heart of Smile Bags are 25 OneWords — a shared set of words that connect environmental science concepts to social emotional well-being. From Absorb to Transform, these words remind us that the same forces that sustain ecosystems — balance, flow, renewal, resilience — are the forces that sustain people too.
