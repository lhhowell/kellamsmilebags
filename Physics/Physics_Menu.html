Spinning Tops Lab

Balance

An engineering journey from CAD to spin test. Explore the AI evaluation, the rubric, and the lesson — then submit your own design for AI feedback.

🎯

In Mr. Acikgoz's physics class, students designed spinning tops in Tinkercad — pushing the limits of weight, balance, sharpness, and symmetry. Some spun beautifully. Some failed in the print bed. Some surprised everyone. Each design became a lesson in physics meeting feeling.

One OneWord shaped this lab: Balance — what holds the spin together. The same word that anchors an ecosystem, anchors a child finding their footing, anchors a top that keeps its axis. Physics meets feeling.

Submit Your Top · AI Feedback

Design a Spinning Top. Get AI Feedback.

Design in Tinkercad. Submit a photo — physical or rendered — for instant AI feedback against the same 4-question rubric used in the lab. Refine and resubmit as many times as you’d like.

Build your top in Tinkercad, then open the Evaluator and submit a photo: either a screenshot of your design or a picture of your printed top. The AI returns a total score out of 12, a predicted spin time, an observation on each criterion, and one specific suggestion for what to refine next. Each submission is logged to the class results.
