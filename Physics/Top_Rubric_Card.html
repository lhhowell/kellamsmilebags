Balance

Spinning Top Rubric · Score each top using these 4 questions

How to use: Look at a top. Score each of the 4 questions: ❌ Needs work (1), ⚠️ Just OK (2), ✅ Looking good (3). Add up the points (max 12) to predict how long it will spin.

1 Where's the weight?

Heavy stuff at the bottom edge stores more spin energy.

❌ TOP-HEAVY
Heavy on top
✅ BOTTOM-HEAVY
Heavy at bottom edge

2 How pointy is the bottom?

A sharp point touches less surface = less friction.

❌ FLAT / WIDE
Wide bottom
✅ SHARP POINT
Tiny tip

3 Is it balanced?

Lopsided = wobble = energy lost to wobble instead of spin.

❌ LOPSIDED
Off-center bits
✅ SYMMETRIC
Same all around

4 Is it smooth?

Bumps and sticking-out parts catch the air and slow it down.

❌ BUMPY / SPIKY
Spikes & gears
✅ STREAMLINED
Smooth shape

Scoring

1❌ Needs work
2⚠️ Just OK
3✅ Looking good
Add up your scores. What's the predicted spin time?
4–6About 1–5 seconds
7–9About 5–15 seconds
10–12About 15–30 seconds
