The Big Idea

A spinning top stays up because it's spinning. The longer it can hold onto its spin, the longer it stays up. Three things steal the spin: friction at the bottom, air pushing on the sides, and wobble from being lopsided. Good designs reduce all three.

That's the OneWord — Balance — at work.

Setup You'll Need

  • The 17 printed tops (PLA, from D210)
  • One test surface everyone uses (glass tile or smooth desk)
  • Stopwatches or phone timers
  • Rubric cards (one per student)
  • Kiosk link or QR code on the board

Fair Test Rules

  • Same surface for every spin
  • Same launch — pinch the stem and twist
  • 3 spins per top, average them
  • If it falls in under 1 second, redo it
  • Time to the first wobble-fall, not the dead stop

For the Redesigners

If you got picked for Round 2: look at where your top scored low on the rubric. Pick one thing to change — a sharper tip, a wider base, less weight on top. Re-print, re-test, see if it spun longer. That's engineering.
