🎯 Essential Question · Pregunta Esencial
¿Cómo hablamos de las preferencias de comida en español?
How do we talk about food preferences in Spanish — using data as our evidence?

📋 Learning Objectives

Students will:

  • Use Spanish vocabulary for foods and drinks accurately.
  • Express likes and dislikes using Me gusta and No me gusta.
  • Ask and answer simple questions using ¿Te gusta…?
  • Read a Spanish menu dataset and use it as evidence in their own sentences.
  • Apply correct articles (el / la) to food vocabulary.

🗣️ Target Language Structures

🔹 Module Breakdown

1
Warm-Up · Calentamiento
5 – 10 minutes

Project this on the screen: "What's the BEST food in the world? ¿Cuál es la mejor comida del mundo?" Cold-call 4–5 students. Ask in English; they answer in Spanish using whatever they know. Write responses on the board with English glosses. Validate any attempt — even "Me gusta hamburger."

Teacher Tip Don't correct article errors yet (la pizza vs el pizza). Goal: participation. Articles come in Module 2.
2
Mini-Lesson · Me gusta / No me gusta
10 – 15 minutes

Write on the board: Me gusta = I like (literally: "It pleases me."). No me gusta = I don't like. Model pronunciation: meh GOOS-tah. Chorus 3 times. Then teach el / la: words ending in -a usually take la; words ending in -o usually take el. Don't dwell on exceptions in Spanish I.

Run 5 rounds of choral practice: ¿Te gusta el pollo? Sí, me gusta. ¿Te gusta la ensalada? No, no me gusta.

3
Vocabulary & Frames · Worksheet Parts 1–2
10 – 15 minutes

Students match each Spanish word with its English meaning (Part 1), then read the sentence frames in Part 2. These frames are the tools they'll use for the rest of the lesson — name them as such: "These are your tools."

Differentiation Heritage / advanced speakers: add 2 of their own foods to the vocabulary list. Beginners: say each word out loud three times with a partner.
4
Partner Interview · Worksheet Part 3
10 – 15 minutes

Pair students. Each writes their partner's name in the blank. Take turns asking the 5 questions and marking ✔️ Sí or ❌ No. Walk-around look-fors: Are students using ¿Te gusta…? in question form? Are they responding in full phrases? Don't correct mid-flow — note for whole-class feedback.

Quick share-out (2 min): cold-call 2 students. "A mi compañera Kayla le gusta la pizza, pero no le gusta el café." (Previews 3rd person — extension only.)

5
Data Activity · Worksheet Parts 4–5
10 – 15 minutes

Now students use the menu data as evidence for their Spanish sentences. This is the bridge between language and analysis. Show how to sort: Data > Sort range > Column E (Popularidad) Z→A for most popular; Column D (Precio) Z→A for most expensive; filter to Bebida + sort Column D A→Z for cheapest drink.

✅ Expected Answers · Respuestas Esperadas
  • 1. La comida más popular es el helado (4.9).
  • 2. La comida más cara es la pizza ($11.49).
  • 3. La bebida más barata es el refresco ($2.29).
Beautiful Moment Watch for students who realize they can use the sentence frames AS-IS — they just slot in the answer. That's the whole point. Frames are training wheels for production.
6
Mi Restaurante · Worksheet Part 6
15 – 20 minutes

Students design their own restaurant menu — pulling items from the dataset (or adding their own Spanish words) — then write 5 sentences about it. Required sentences:

  • En mi restaurante hay _______, _______, y _______.
  • Me gusta el / la _______ porque _______.
  • No me gusta el / la _______ porque _______.
  • Elijo _______ porque es popular. ← uses dataset evidence
  • La comida más cara en mi menú es _______.
7
Exit Ticket · Boleto de Salida
5 minutes

Students write one thing they like and one thing they don't like, in Spanish. Collect as exit ticket. Strong responses use the full sentence frame: Me gusta la pizza. No me gusta el café.

🟢🔵 Differentiation & Extensions

🟢 Support
  • Provide a printed vocabulary list with images.
  • Pre-fill the article (el/la) in worksheet blanks.
  • Pair beginner with proficient partner for Part 3.
  • Use the kiosk as a Spanish-only "answer checker."
🔵 Extension · Heritage Speakers
  • Add 5 foods of their own to the dataset in Spanish.
  • Use 3rd person: A mi compañera le gusta…
  • Write 3 reasons in Spanish for menu choices.
  • Translate one classmate's menu into English.
